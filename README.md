Before making an offer, conduct thorough legal due diligence on the property. Ensure all documentation is in order and check the following:

- Occupancy Certificate: Ensures the flat is ready for habitation.
- Building Approvals: Verify that the construction adheres to local regulations.
- Home Loan Eligibility: If financing, ensure the property is eligible for a loan from your bank.

Consider hiring a legal expert to assist with this phase, as it can prevent potential headaches in the future.
